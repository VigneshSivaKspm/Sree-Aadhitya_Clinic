1. HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open ChatGPT (image generation enabled). Start a NEW chat per section so the
   style stays consistent inside that section.
2. For each image: paste the MASTER PREFIX (section 3), then the image's own
   prompt, then the "Size line" from that prompt. Example:
       [MASTER PREFIX]  +  [PROMPT A1]  +  "Generate at 1536x1024 (landscape)."
3. ChatGPT only produces a few native sizes:
       Square     1024 x 1024   (1:1)
       Landscape  1536 x 1024   (3:2)
       Portrait   1024 x 1536   (2:3)
   So each prompt below tells you (a) what to GENERATE at and (b) what to CROP
   the result to. The website crops with "object-cover", so always keep the
   important subject near the CENTRE with breathing room at the edges.
4. If the first result is off, follow up with short edits, e.g.
       "Same image, but warmer light and less clutter."
       "Move the subject slightly left; keep the right third empty."
       "Remove any text or logos that appear."
5. Generate 2-3 variations for the important images (hero images) and pick the
   calmest, least cluttered one.
6. Finished files are uploaded from the ADMIN panel (see the "Upload via" line
   on every image). No developer help is needed to swap images later.

2. GLOBAL STYLE GUIDE, PALETTE AND SAFETY RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BRAND FEEL (both hospitals): trust, cleanliness, calm, premium, modern,
authentic, accessible. Restrained - never flashy, never "stocky", never noisy.

LOOK
  - Photorealistic editorial photography (unless a prompt says "illustration").
  - Soft, natural daylight; gentle shadows; shallow depth of field.
  - Uncluttered compositions with generous empty space (text sits next to images
    on the site, and pictures get cropped on phones).
  - No text, letters, numbers, logos, signage, watermarks or UI in any image.
  - Landscape images: subject centred or on the left/right third with calm space.

PALETTE (matches the website theme; use these words/hex codes in follow-ups)
  Main brand ink ........ deep green-ink  #14332E
  Warm paper/sand ....... #F4F0E7  /  #FAF8F3
  AYURVEDA greens ....... #3B5729 (deep), #5F883F (mid), #A3C085 (soft), #E3ECD9 (pale)
  Turmeric accent ....... #C98A1B
  DENTAL coral ..... #A2343A (deep), #C8434A (main-dark), #F17577 (BRAND CORAL),
                         #F58F91 (light), #FDE3E3 (pale), #FEF3F3 (tint)
  Neutrals .............. clean white, soft grey, natural wood, linen

AYURVEDA MOOD: natural, warm, herbal, earthy, serene. Elements: fresh green
leaves, banana leaf, clay and brass vessels, wooden trays, linen, marigold,
turmeric, herbs, warm oils, soft morning light, muted greens + warm neutrals.

DENTAL MOOD: clean, bright, modern, gentle. Elements: white surfaces, glass,
soft coral-red accents, tidy clinical rooms, smiling (natural, not exaggerated)
patients, bright daylight. Avoid anything frightening.

SAFETY / COMPLIANCE RULES (important - this is a healthcare website)
  - NO medical claims in images: no "before/after", no dramatic cures, no
    glowing "healing" effects, no pain/injury imagery, no blood, no close-up
    needles or drills pointed at a face.
  - NO invented credentials: no certificates, awards, badges, stethoscope-with-
    text, "100%" or "best" signage.
  - NO real brands, logos or readable packaging text. Product packaging must be
    plain and label-less (real labels get added later).
  - PEOPLE: AI-generated faces must NEVER be presented as the hospital's real
    doctors, staff or patients. For doctor photos use REAL photos supplied by
    the hospital. The doctor prompts in section G are neutral placeholders
    (illustrated / back-view) meant to be replaced.
  - Where possible show hands, silhouettes, or people from behind/side, and
    keep faces soft and non-identifiable.
  - Culturally respectful, authentic Indian context (South Indian setting is
    fine). No caricatures, no stereotyped costumes.
  - Do not present any treatment as guaranteed. Images should feel calm and
    informational, not promotional.
